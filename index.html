Beginner reminders

  • Warm up for 4–5 minutes before training.
  • Start with 2 sets per exercise for the first two weeks.
  • Rest 60–120 seconds between sets.
  • Finish most sets feeling you could do 2–3 more good reps.
  • Prioritise regular meals, protein and 7–9 hours of sleep.
  • Stop if an exercise causes sharp pain.
